Rules

  • Each player has 9 points (the 3×3 interior corners of their quadrant) and plays exactly one per round.
  • Each round a master point is drawn at random (without replacement) from the 49 interior corners of the whole board, and is visible to everyone before they pick. You may not play directly on it unless it's your only point left.
  • Your score for the round is the smallest distance from your point to any of the other 4 points in play (3 opponents + master).
  • If the master point falls strictly inside one player's region, that player's own distance is tripled. On the shared border between two regions, both of those players get +1 added to their distance instead. The single center point shared by all four is neutral.
  • Picks are hidden until everyone is ready, then revealed together.
  • Lowest sum over all 9 rounds wins.
